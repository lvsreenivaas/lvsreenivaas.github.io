What I actually do

Three habits show up in every project on this site.

Count the independent observations first

A sixteen-year regime variable with five episodes has an effective sample size of five, no matter how many funds you stack underneath it. Most of the "significant" results I've chased died here, and finding out why was the contribution.

Go to the primary source

Public pension funds publish their entire private-equity book quarterly, in PDFs, for free. Commercial vendors resell a smoothed, sparser version of it for six figures. Building the parser was the easy part; knowing it was worth building was the work.

Diagnose the failure, don't just report it

When the deep graph model stopped working, the useful finding wasn't that it stopped — it was that prediction dispersion collapsed 20× while train and test correlations stayed identical. That's oversmoothing, not overfitting, and it points at a different fix.
