Privacy policy

Short version: this is a personal site. I collect as little as possible, I don't sell anything, and the only data that reaches me is what you choose to send.

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Who runs this site

This site is operated by Venkata Sreenivaas Lingam as a personal portfolio. You can reach me at venkata_sl@berkeley.edu about anything on this page.

Hosting

The site is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub records standard server access logs, which include visitor IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. I have no access to those logs and cannot retrieve, export or delete them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. IP anonymisation is enabled, so your address is truncated before storage. Analytics tells me aggregate things — which write-ups get read, roughly where readers are — and nothing that identifies you personally.

If you'd rather not be counted, any of these works: browser "Do Not Track" or tracking-protection settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out browser add-on. Nothing on this site is gated behind analytics, so blocking it costs you nothing.

Analytics is loaded only when a measurement ID is configured. If none is set, no analytics script loads and no analytics cookies are written.

Cookies

I set no cookies of my own. Google Analytics, when active, sets its standard first-party cookies (_ga and related) to distinguish repeat visits. There is no advertising, no remarketing, no cross-site tracking, and nothing is shared with data brokers.

The contact form

The form on the contact page is processed by Formspree, which receives what you type — your name, email address, subject and message — and forwards it to my inbox. I use it to reply to you, and for nothing else. I don't add you to a mailing list, because I don't have one.

Formspree's handling of submissions is governed by its own privacy policy. If you'd rather skip the third party entirely, email me directly at venkata_sl@berkeley.edu.

Messages stay in my email. I'll delete yours on request — just ask.

External links and embeds

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, SSRN and similar sites take you to services with their own privacy practices, which I don't control. This site embeds no third-party fonts, no social widgets and no trackers beyond the analytics described above; every asset other than the analytics script is served from this domain.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — the EEA, the UK and California all have relevant law — you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data relating to you, and to object to its processing. Given how little this site holds, that usually amounts to deleting an email you sent me. Write to venkata_sl@berkeley.edu and I'll handle it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes I'll update the date at the top. There's no version history worth maintaining for a page this short.
