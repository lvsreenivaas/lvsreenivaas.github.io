Currently reading

Thinking in Bets

Annie Duke

Duke's central separation is between decision quality and outcome quality — a good decision can lose and a terrible one can win, and conflating the two ("resulting") is how people learn the wrong lesson from their own track record.

It lands hard in quantitative research, where feedback is slow, noisy and arrives long after you had to commit. Most of the projects on this site are cases where the outcome looked fine and the decision process was the thing that needed auditing — a significant p-value from a null that was never defensible is a won hand played badly.
